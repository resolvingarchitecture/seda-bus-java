- [ ] 🔴 **The public contract is documented.** Javadoc covers semantics, but per-operation errors, nullability and thread-safety are not stated for publish, send or registerChannel. **Fix:** Document inputs, failure returns and thread-safety for each public method.

- [ ] 🔴 **Thread-safety is stated for every shared component.** DESIGN.md concurrency notes are general; thread-safety is not stated per component or method. **Fix:** State the thread-safety of SEDABus, SEDAMessageChannel and the consumer callbacks.

**Observability** — _low_

Gaps:
